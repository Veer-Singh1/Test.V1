## Decisions I made

- **"Part of why you're solid"** is kept as written. It's accurate: single atoms are held open by the uncertainty principle, and whole objects being solid also relies on the Pauli exclusion principle (Dyson and Lenard 1967; Lieb and Thirring 1975).
- **"Fly right back out"** is a cartoon picture of the real argument: squeeze the electron and its kinetic energy rises, so it can't sit on the nucleus.
- **Length:** your script runs about 25 s; I kept it.
