    About this tribute

    A portrait made of moments: films, a speech, a book club, a dress made of bottles.

    Scatter it and every tile becomes something to read. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to travel through the cloud, and tap a tile to bring it forward.

    Every image here is a labelled placeholder until it is replaced with a photo the site’s owner took or is licensed to use. Facts come from the public record, and every quote is from her HeForShe speech at the United Nations on 20 September 2014.
