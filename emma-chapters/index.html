Prologue

Born in Paris on 15 April 1990 and raised in Oxfordshire, Emma Watson was nine when she was cast as Hermione Granger. She grew up on screen, studied English literature at Brown University and became a UN Women Goodwill Ambassador.

    Chapter III

    The Reader

    Book clubs, notes and the London Underground

    Books & reading

    A shelf shared with strangers

    In January 2016 Emma Watson started Our Shared Shelf, a feminist book club that met online. Its first pick was Gloria Steinem's My Life on the Road.

    That November she hid copies of Maya Angelou's Mom & Me & Mom around the London Underground for commuters to find, each with a handwritten note, working with Books on the Underground.

    Her own degree, from Brown University in 2014, is in English literature.
