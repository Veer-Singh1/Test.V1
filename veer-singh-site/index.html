01 — THE FIGHTER'S FILE

BRAINS & BRAWN

I'm Veer Singh: fifteen, restless, and allergic to average. I believe the sharpest people in the room should also be the hardest to knock down, so I train both: the mind in the books, the body in the gym.

In the classroom I'm the one asking "but why?" until the answer actually makes sense. Right now that curiosity is pointed at Python, quantitative finance and crypto markets: turning numbers into decisions.

Outside it, I'm a student of combat sports. MMA taught me that talent opens the door, but discipline is what keeps you standing in the championship rounds. Same rule applies to everything else.
