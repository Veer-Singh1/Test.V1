# AI image prompts for the window

The site works without these: every panel is already drawn in code. Each image you make replaces the drawn glass in one panel, lit from behind by the moving sun.

## How to make them

- Generate each one **separately**, so you can pick the best take of each.
- **Shape:** portrait 4:5 for the six panels, square 1:1 for the rose window. At least 1024 px wide.
- **Paste the style line first,** then the panel's own line, so all seven images match.
- Keep the design **flat and front-on**, filling the frame edge to edge, with **no wall, frame, people's faces, text or watermark**.
- Save them as `panel-1.jpg` … `panel-6.jpg` and `rose.jpg` and send them to me. I'll drop them in.

## Style line (start every prompt with this)

> A single medieval stained-glass window panel photographed flat and front-on, lit brilliantly from behind by sunlight, filling the entire frame edge to edge. Thick black lead lines, rich jewel-toned hand-blown glass with visible streaks, seeds and bubbles, a decorative border band with small round roundels, a circular medallion in the centre. Gothic cathedral glass in the style of Chartres and Sainte-Chapelle. No wall, no window frame, no text, no lettering, no human faces, no watermark.

## The rose window (square)

7. **rose.** Replace the style line's "single panel" wording with this full prompt:

> A circular Gothic rose window seen flat and front-on, lit brilliantly from behind, filling the entire square frame with the black background visible only in the corners. Thirteen petal-shaped lancets radiating from a small golden centre, each petal a different jewel colour (sapphire, ruby, emerald, amethyst, amber, rose), thick black lead lines, delicate stone tracery between the petals, hand-blown glass with streaks and bubbles. Gothic cathedral glass in the style of Notre-Dame de Paris. No text, no human faces, no watermark.

## Tips

- If a result has a frame or wall around it, crop it out before sending. The glass should touch all four edges.
- Bright, saturated results look best, because the page dims and lights the glass itself.
- Avoid prompts that ask for a likeness of Emma Watson or any real person. The panels tell her story through symbols, the way real church windows do.
