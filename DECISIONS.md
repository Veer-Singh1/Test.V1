## Brand and content
- **"Manifest Coffee Roasters" is an invented business.** The name, team (first
  names only), prices, phone number and email are all placeholders. **[review]**
  Before launch, check that the name is free to use in South Africa.
- Email uses the reserved `.example` domain (`hello@manifestroasters.example`)
  and the phone number is `+27 31 000 0000`, so nothing points at a real
  inbox or line. Replace both before launch.
- The address is "Unit 4, The Bond Store, off Maydon Wharf Road, Durban".
  The road is real and harbour-side, so the story holds up. The building
  name is invented and there's no street number. The parking note mentions
  a car guard called Bheki. **[review]** Replace these with real details.
- "Week 47" and the roast dates (17, 19 and 24 Nov) in the manifest are
  fixed text, matching the week this was built. If the coffee list is
  maintained by hand, those dates need updating with it, or they can be
  removed.
- The founding story (popcorn popper, Glenwood garage, Florida Road café)
  and the "price paid" example (2.6 times the commodity price) are written
  to sound true. They are placeholders and must be replaced with real facts.
- Prices are in rand and set around 2026 Durban speciality levels (R185–R260
  per 250 g). Adjust to your actual pricing.
- Local words like *howzit*, *lekker*, *naartjie* and *now-now* are used
  sparingly so the copy feels local without turning into a joke.
- Origins (Ethiopia, Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda, Uganda) and their ports of
  loading are plausible but simplified. Ethiopian coffee really does ship out
  through Djibouti. The voyage chart is an **illustration, not an accurate
  map**.

## Technical
- **The site is in `site/`**, not the repository root, because the root
  already holds an unrelated quant-finance starter project and I didn't want
  to mix them. The design documents (`DESIGN.md`, `DECISIONS.md`,
  `SUMMARY.md`) are at the root so you can find them.
- **Fonts are self-hosted** in `site/fonts/` (copied from the `@fontsource` npm
  packages; all are under the SIL Open Font License, and the licence files are
  included). This means no Google Fonts request, no layout shift from a
  third-party server, and the site works offline.
- **Screenshots are JPEG**, not PNG. The existing `.gitignore` excludes
  `*.png`, and the paper-grain texture makes PNGs very large. I left
  `.gitignore` as it was.
- **The roaster status pill** in the header uses the visitor's clock
  converted to Durban time (`Africa/Johannesburg`) and the opening hours in
  the copy. It's hidden below 1080 px wide so it doesn't crowd the menu.
- **The voyage chart** uses `position: sticky` plus a scroll listener
  throttled to one update per animation frame, and only runs while its
  section is near the viewport. No scroll library is used. On phones the
  chart's view follows the ship, because the whole coastline at phone width
  would make the labels about 5 px tall.
- **Links from Services pre-tick the matching box on the form**, for example
  `contact.html?enquiry=wholesale`.
- **The phone price table becomes stacked rows** (below 640 px) using CSS
  grid on the table rows. Some browsers drop table semantics when table
  rows change their `display`, so the table also has explicit ARIA table
  roles (`role="row"`, `role="cell"` and so on) to keep it a table for
  screen readers.
- **Accessibility tooling:** axe-core 4.10 reports 0 violations. The only
  items it can't decide automatically are contrast checks over textured
  backgrounds, which I verified by hand (see `DESIGN.md`).
- The contact form has **no backend**. Submitting it validates the fields,
  shows a "RECEIVED" stamp and announces success to screen readers. You'll
  need to hook it up to a form service or your own endpoint. **[review]**
- The header and footer are copied into each of the four pages instead of
  being generated by a templating step. At four pages, keeping the site
  build-free is worth a little duplication. If you edit the nav, change all
  four files.
