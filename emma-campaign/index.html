03 / Biography

Every detail holds.

Emma Charlotte Duerre Watson was born in Paris on 15 April 1990 and raised in Oxfordshire, England. She was cast as Hermione Granger at nine and played her in all eight Harry Potter films.

Alongside her film work she studied English literature, then turned her public profile toward gender equality, reading and sustainable fashion.

  • EducationBA in English literature, Brown University, 2014
  • United NationsUN Women Goodwill Ambassador from 2014
  • AwardWon the first gender-neutral MTV Movie Award for Best Actor in a Movie, 2017
  • BoardJoined the board of directors of Kering, 2020

Plays Sam in The Perks of Being a Wallflower.

Graduates from Brown University. Named UN Women Goodwill Ambassador and launches HeForShe at the UN on 20 September.

Starts the feminist book club Our Shared Shelf.

2017

Plays Belle in Beauty and the Beast.

Donates £1 million to the UK Justice and Equality Fund, supporting the Time's Up UK movement.

2019

Plays Meg March in Greta Gerwig's Little Women.

2020

Joins the board of the luxury group Kering.
