Story

Emma Watson was born in Paris on 15 April 1990 and grew up in Oxfordshire, England.

At nine she was cast as Hermione Granger, the girl who always did the reading. She played her across all eight Harry Potter films, from 2001 to 2011, and became the face of a generation that was proud to be clever.

Between films she kept studying, and in 2014 she graduated from Brown University with a degree in English literature.

The same year she stood up at the United Nations and asked men to join the fight for gender equality. Since then her work has moved between the screen, the stage of global advocacy and the quiet power of a good book.

Library

Hermione would be proud. Emma's book club opened in January 2016 with Gloria Steinem's My Life on the Road.

Later that year she hid copies of Maya Angelou's Mom & Me & Mom around the London Underground for strangers to find, each with a handwritten note.
