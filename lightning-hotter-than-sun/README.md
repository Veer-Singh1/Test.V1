# Is lightning hotter than the Sun? (Physics Shorts kit)

Finished videos:
- `is_lightning_hotter_than_the_sun.mp4`: 1080×1920, 30 fps, 57.6 s, your ElevenLabs voiceover plus "Idea 10", −14 LUFS.
- `is_lightning_hotter_than_the_sun_original_music.mp4`: the same, with the original royalty-free bed.

Script (transcribed from your audio), picture notes, fact check and a suggested SEO pack are in `script.md`.

- `render.bat` renders `final.mp4` on Windows; `test_render.bat` renders a quick test.
- To swap in a different take: convert it to `public/voiceover.wav`, run `python scripts/align_vo.py public/voiceover.wav src/script.json`, rebuild the music and mix (see `../clouds-white-sky-blue/README.md`), then render.
