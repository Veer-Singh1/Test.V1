Wholesale, step by step

From cupping table to counter

Most cafés are up and running within two weeks. Nobody signs anything until you've tasted the coffee on your own machine.

  1. 01Week 1

    Cupping at the roastery

    Bring your team. We'll taste the espresso blend and two single origins, and talk about what your customers like to drink.

  2. 02Week 2

    Dial-in day

    Sipho comes to your café, sets up your grinder and trains your team on your own machine.

  3. 03Every week

    Roast & deliver

    Order by Monday noon. We roast on Tuesday and deliver on Wednesday, anywhere from Umhlanga to Hillcrest.

  4. 04Every quarter

    Check-ins & retraining

    We bring menu ideas and seasonal coffees, retrain new staff, and come back when summer humidity throws your grind off.

Questions

Frequently asked at the counter

Do you deliver to Ballito, Hillcrest or PMB?

Ballito and Hillcrest are on our Wednesday and Friday van runs, and delivery is free over R400. Pietermaritzburg goes by courier for R75 and usually arrives the next day.

Which grind should I choose?

Choose whole bean if you have a grinder, because it stays fresh for longer. If you don't, tell us your brewer (plunger, moka pot, AeroPress, pour-over or espresso machine) and we'll grind it to suit.

Can I pause my Standing Order?

Yes. WhatsApp us before Sunday night and we'll skip the next delivery. There's no contract and no cancellation fee.

What's the minimum for wholesale?

5 kg a week for an ongoing account. Smaller offices usually do better with a Captain Standing Order.

Is the Saturday cupping really free?

It is. It runs from 9am for about an hour, with room for twelve people. Booking helps us put out enough cups, but walk-ins are welcome.

Not sure which berth?

Tell us how you drink coffee and roughly how much you go through, and we'll suggest one. No sales pitch.
