Port of call

Unit 4, The Bond Store

Off Maydon Wharf Road, Durban
29°52′S 31°01′E

Coming from town, follow the signs for the harbour and Maydon Wharf, then look for the rust-coloured roller door with a big stencilled M. There are two bays out front and plenty of parking on the street. Bheki, the car guard, will look after you, so say hi.
