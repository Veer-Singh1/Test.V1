# Fact check: Antwerp diamond heist test script

Step 2 of the pipeline. Every number, name and date in `script.json`, with where it stands.
Checked by web search on 14 Nov 2026. Wikipedia itself could not be opened from the build
machine, so these rest on search summaries of the sources below. Please read it once yourself
before publishing.

| Claim in the script | Status | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heist took place in February 2003, over the weekend of 15 to 16 February | Confirmed | Several sources agree |
| Antwerp Diamond Centre, Antwerp, Belgium | Confirmed | |
| "More than 100 million dollars" stolen | Confirmed | Sources say "more than $100 million". It is an estimate |
| "One of the biggest diamond heists in history" | Confirmed | Guinness lists it as the largest. The script uses the safer wording |
| "10 layers of security" | Commonly reported | Repeated in most accounts. Not checked against a primary source |
| "More than 100 boxes" emptied | Confirmed, wording chosen on purpose | Sources disagree: 123 of 160, 109 of 189, or "100". "More than 100" fits all of them |
| Notarbartolo rented an office in the building "for years" | Confirmed, wording chosen on purpose | Sources say two or three years |
| Vault "deep under the building" | Partly confirmed | Sources say basement or subterranean. The blueprint prop says "LEVEL -2", which I could not confirm |
| "Most of the world's rough diamonds pass through this city" | Not checked this session | Widely quoted (about 80%) for the early 2000s. Verify or soften |
| Sensors, magnets and lock defeated | Commonly reported | Detail comes mainly from one 2009 magazine account |
| Trash dumped in woods by a highway, found by a local man who called police | Confirmed | Landowner named Van Camp. One source instead says it was thrown from the getaway car |
| Bag held Diamond Centre envelopes and a half-eaten salami sandwich | Confirmed | |
| DNA from the sandwich linked Notarbartolo | Confirmed | |
| Arrested "days later" | Confirmed | 21 February 2003 |
| Sentenced to 10 years | Confirmed | Sources differ on the year of sentencing, so the script gives none |
| "Three of his crew get 5 years each" | Confirmed | |
| Diamonds never found | Confirmed | |
