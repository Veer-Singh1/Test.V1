# Fact check: the Brink's job

Step 2 of the pipeline: every number, name and date in `script.json`, with where it stands.
Checked by web search on 14 Nov 2026. fbi.gov and Wikipedia could not be opened from the build
machine, so these rest on search summaries of the sources listed at the bottom. Please read it
once yourself before publishing.

**Status key.** *Confirmed*: at least one source in the searches states it, and none contradicts
it. *Commonly reported*: widely repeated, but I did not see it confirmed this session.
*Commentary*: a line of narration, not a factual claim.

## Lines that are commentary, not fact

"Its whole job is keeping other people's money safe", "This isn't a smash and grab", "Every visit
is a risk", "It's slow. It's risky. And it works.", "In jail, there's a lot of time to think" and
similar lines are narration, not claims.

## Props in the animation

- **The newspaper** uses an invented masthead ("The Evening Edition") and invented headlines.
  It is not a real paper.
- **The book cover** shows the real title of O'Keefe's book.
- **The Brink's sign** on the building and truck is plain text, not the company's logo.
