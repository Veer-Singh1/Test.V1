## Description (long video)

In the year 793, English chroniclers recorded "fiery dragons flying across the firmament", just before the Vikings struck Lindisfarne. So what did medieval people actually believe a dragon was?

This video covers bestiaries that said a dragon's power was in its tail, Beowulf's fire-breathing dragon and the one scorched manuscript it survives in, Fáfnir and Sigurd, St George and the Golden Legend, the red dragon of Wales, the Wawel Dragon of Kraków, a woolly rhinoceros skull displayed as a dragon's, and the one object that really says "here be dragons".
